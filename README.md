# Simple Recipe Cookbook

You can view this book directly here:

https://johnnyb.github.io/cookbook/MainBook.html

I like simple recipes.
Most cookbooks over-complicate things.
The goal here is to present the minimum recipe and procedure in order to make it easy to make, easy to remember, and easy to modify.

The initial book came out of ChatGPT, but mostly at my direction for recipes.
Occasionally I asked ChatGPT to produce a recipe I wanted to try or was too lazy to type out or to copy a recipe from a website.

To the extent possible, I've written this in semantic HTML.
The initial HTML rendering and stylesheet is straight from ChatGPT, and I think it did a pretty good job.

In any case, the goal is to give a starting point for recipes that are easy to remember and customize.

What tends to happen is that I'll cook something continually for three months and then forget about it for six months, and then when I come back to it six months later I've forgotten all the important pieces.
The goal of this manual is to help me remember them!
